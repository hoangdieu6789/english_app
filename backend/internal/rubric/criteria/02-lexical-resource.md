# Lexical Resource (LR)

## What it measures

The range of vocabulary and how precisely and appropriately it is used: word choice, collocation, less common and idiomatic items, style, and the ability to paraphrase when the right word is not known.

## Features to check

| Code | Feature | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| LR-01 | Range: enough vocabulary for familiar and unfamiliar topics, at length | variety of words, topic-specific vocabulary, repetition of the same basic words (*good, nice, very*) |
| LR-02 | Precision and appropriacy: word choice, collocation, style | wrong word (*make a photo*), wrong collocation (*strong rain*), wrong register |
| LR-03 | Less common and idiomatic items | *a steep learning curve, it's not my cup of tea, keen on, cut down on*; used naturally, not forced |
| LR-04 | Paraphrase | explaining a word the learner doesn't know (*the thing you use to… / a kind of…*), rewording the question instead of repeating it |

## Band descriptors

| Band | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| 9 | Total flexibility and precise use in all contexts. Sustained use of accurate and idiomatic language. |
| 8 | Wide resource, readily and flexibly used to discuss all topics and convey precise meaning. Skilful use of less common and idiomatic items despite occasional inaccuracies in word choice and collocation. Effective use of paraphrase as required. |
| 7 | Resource flexibly used to discuss a variety of topics. Some ability to use less common and idiomatic items, and an awareness of style and collocation is evident, though inappropriacies occur. Effective use of paraphrase as required. |
| 6 | Resource sufficient to discuss topics at length. Vocabulary use may be inappropriate, but meaning is clear. Generally able to paraphrase successfully. |
| 5 | Resource sufficient to discuss familiar and unfamiliar topics, but with limited flexibility. Attempts paraphrase, but not always successfully. |
| 4 | Resource sufficient for familiar topics, but only basic meaning can be conveyed on unfamiliar topics. Frequent inappropriacies and errors in word choice. Rarely attempts paraphrase. |
| 3 | Resource limited to simple vocabulary used mainly to convey personal information. Vocabulary inadequate for unfamiliar topics. |
| 2 | Very limited resource. Utterances consist of isolated words or memorised utterances. Little communication possible without mime or gesture. |
| 1 | No resource bar a few isolated words. No communication possible. |
| 0 | Does not attend / does not complete. |

## Grading notes

- Idioms must be **natural and accurate in context**. Memorised idioms inserted where they don't fit (*It's raining cats and dogs* about light rain) are inappropriacies, not evidence for band 7+.
- Judge the topic: a learner with good range on a familiar Part 1 topic may only show band 5 on an abstract Part 3 topic. Rate the average.
- Word-form errors (*I am very interesting in music*) belong to LR if the wrong word is chosen; if it is an inflection error (*two book*), put it under GRA. Do not double-count.
- Spelling does not exist in speech; ignore ASR spelling variants.
- Typical issues for Vietnamese learners: over-use of *very + basic adjective*; direct translation collocations (*learn knowledge*, *play with friends* for adults socialising); *make/do*, *say/tell* confusion.
